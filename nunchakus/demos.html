Nunchaku Demonstrations

Maxi's weapon exhibition in real life

Maxi is a character from the fighting game series Soulcalibur. He made his first appearance in the second game in the series, Soulcalibur. The video game clip was recorded on an Elgato 4K S on an Xbox 360, running the Xbox edition of Soulcalibur II. This was quite difficult to learn. The fastest parts of the entire kata are the first 5 seconds, 14 seconds in when he passes the nunchakus behind his neck, and 20 seconds in when he spins them up over his shoulder.

Maxi's double nunchaku kata in real life

The double nunchaku demonstration is from Soulcalibur. Interestingly, this exhibition is not present in any other game in the series. The entire kata is done at a relatively breakneck speed; a lot of force is required to rapidly change the nunchakus' direction of motion.
